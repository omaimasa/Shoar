How SMS Consent is Collected (English)

When a user visits shoar.com.sa and clicks "كتابة تقييم" (Write a Review) or "إضافة متجر" (Add Store), a modal appears requesting their Saudi phone number.

The modal displays the following consent language directly below the phone number input field:

"بإدخال رقمك توافق على استلام رسالة SMS للتحقق من هويتك. لن نرسل رسائل تسويقية"
(Translation: "By entering your number, you agree to receive an SMS for identity verification. No marketing messages will be sent.")
